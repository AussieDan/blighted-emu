Left-drag turns, right-drag moves, wheel zooms. Click a player to follow them. R puts the camera back behind the followed player. The ground is coloured by height and slope (sand, grass, highland, bare peaks, cliffs) out to the horizon. Light outlines = rocks, buildings and props. Green cones = trees and bushes. Yellow = followed player, blue = player, grey = bot, red = mob, faded = dead. Lines: attacker → target. Blue sheet = the sea.
