How to read the percent. The percent is an estimate. It tells how much of the retail feature works with the real game client on our server. It is not a count of lines of code. A percent is never higher than its proof: Code at most 30 %, Tests at most 60 %, Bots at most 80 %. Only Live can go higher. Tools that the client does not use (setup, game data, capture decoders, admin tools) are measured by their own checks.
